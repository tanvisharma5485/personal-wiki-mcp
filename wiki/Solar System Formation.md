# Solar System Formation

The Solar System formed from a rotating protoplanetary disk around the young Sun.

## Key Points

* Dust grew into planetesimals and planetary embryos.
* Collisions, gas capture, migration, resonances, and scattering produced the present architecture.
* Temperature gradients favored rock and metal in the inner system and more volatile-rich material farther out.
