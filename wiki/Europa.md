# Europa

Europa is an ice-covered moon of Jupiter with strong evidence for a global salty subsurface ocean.

## Key Points

* Its ocean makes it a central example of water beyond Earth.
* The ice shell, tidal forcing, chemistry, and radiation environment all matter to its habitability assessment.
* Multiple observation methods are needed to separate direct evidence from model-dependent interpretation.
