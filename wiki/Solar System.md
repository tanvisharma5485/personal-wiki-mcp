# Solar System

The Solar System is best understood as an interconnected physical system whose bodies reflect formation, composition, gravity, thermal evolution, orbital dynamics, surface change, atmospheres, magnetism, observation, and exploration.

## Key Points

* Present-day properties must be separated from formation history and later evolution.
* Equivalent quantities must be compared: a giant planet's visible cloud level is not a solid surface.
* Reliable conclusions usually combine imaging, spectroscopy, gravity, magnetic, thermal, radar, and dynamical evidence.
* [[Planetary Classification]] separates terrestrial and giant planets, dwarf planets, satellites, and small bodies so equivalent kinds of objects can be compared.
