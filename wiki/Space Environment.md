# Space Environment

The interplanetary environment contains solar radiation, solar wind plasma, energetic particles, gas, and dust.

## Key Points

* Solar activity drives space weather throughout the heliosphere.
* Cosmic rays and solar energetic particles contribute to radiation environments.
* Interplanetary dust produces micrometeorites, meteors, meteor showers, and zodiacal light under different circumstances.
