# Orbital Dynamics

Orbital dynamics explains Solar System architecture through gravity and the exchange of energy and angular momentum.

## Key Points

* Elliptical orbits, perturbations, and gravitational assists follow from Newtonian gravity and Keplerian motion.
* Resonances can stabilize configurations or increase eccentricity.
* Tides can alter rotation and orbits, produce tidal locking, and heat interiors.
