# Planetary Classification

Solar System bodies are organized by physical character and dynamical context, which makes comparisons more meaningful than treating every object as the same kind of world.

## Key Points

* [[Terrestrial Planets]]—Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars—are rock- and metal-rich bodies with solid surfaces.
* [[Giant Planets]] include the hydrogen-helium gas giants Jupiter and Saturn and the ice giants Uranus and Neptune, whose visible exteriors are cloud levels rather than solid ground.
* [[Dwarf Planets]], [[Natural Satellites]], [[Asteroids]], and [[Comets]] are distinct but scientifically connected populations; their present locations and properties reflect formation, migration, collision, and orbital evolution.
* The inner system formed where refractory rock and metal dominated, while ices increased the solid material available farther out and helped giant-planet cores grow and capture gas.
