# Space Exploration

Planetary exploration combines remote sensing, geophysical measurements, in-situ investigation, and sample analysis.

## Key Points

* Flybys provide snapshots, while orbiters enable repeated mapping.
* Landers and rovers directly measure local environments.
* Sample return enables detailed laboratory study unavailable to spacecraft instruments.
