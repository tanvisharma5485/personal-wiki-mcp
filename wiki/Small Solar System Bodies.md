# Small Solar System Bodies

Small Solar System bodies connect the surviving material of planet formation with present-day collisional, orbital, and impact processes.

## Key Points

* [[Asteroids]] range from carbon-rich to silicate-rich and metal-rich classes and occupy main-belt, Trojan, and near-Earth populations.
* [[Comets]] are volatile-rich bodies whose solar-heated activity produces comae and dust and ion tails.
* The [[Outer Solar System Reservoirs]] supply important distant populations, while near-Earth objects connect small-body science with [[Planetary Defense]].
* Individual source topics include Vesta and Pallas, Halley’s Comet and comet 67P, and trans-Neptunian objects such as Sedna.
