# Enceladus

Enceladus is an icy moon of Saturn with a global subsurface ocean.

## Key Points

* South-polar jets eject water-rich material into space.
* The plumes provide an observational path to material from the hidden ocean.
* Tidal heating links its orbit to continuing geologic activity.
