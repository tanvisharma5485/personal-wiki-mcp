# Planetary Evolution

Planetary evolution connects formation with later changes in interiors, surfaces, atmospheres, water inventories, and magnetic fields.

## Key Points

* Accretion, giant impacts, differentiation, and migration established early structure.
* Residual formation heat, radioactive heating, and tidal heating can drive later activity.
* Impacts, volcanism, tectonics, erosion, atmospheric escape, and resurfacing make worlds diverge over time.
