# Comparative Planetology

Comparative planetology explains differences among worlds by applying the same physical dimensions and processes to each.

## Key Points

* Useful comparisons include size, mass, density, composition, atmosphere, surface, gravity, magnetism, rotation, orbit, moons, rings, water, energy balance, and geologic history.
* Mechanisms are more informative than isolated rankings or numbers.
* Measurements must refer to equivalent levels and conditions, especially for temperature and giant-planet surfaces.
