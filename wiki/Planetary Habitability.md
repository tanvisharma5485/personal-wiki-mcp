# Planetary Habitability

Planetary habitability asks whether an environment can sustain conditions compatible with life, while the search for life requires observable evidence.

## Key Points

* Water alone is insufficient; energy, chemistry, stability, radiation, and long-term persistence also matter.
* A circumstellar habitable zone describes one constraint and does not establish that a world is inhabited.
* Biosignatures must be interpreted using environmental context and multiple lines of evidence.
