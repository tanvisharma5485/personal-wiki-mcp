# Dwarf Planets

Dwarf planets are rounded planetary bodies that orbit the Sun but have not dynamically cleared their orbital neighborhoods.

## Key Points

* The source set treats Pluto, Ceres, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake as important examples.
* Dwarf planets occupy both the asteroid belt and the trans-Neptunian region.
* Their surfaces and compositions preserve evidence about volatile transport, water-bearing material, collisions, and early Solar System evolution.
* Pluto and Ceres have dedicated notes because the corpus provides reusable physical details; Eris, Haumea, and Makemake are retained here because their source files provide names but no distinct factual descriptions.
