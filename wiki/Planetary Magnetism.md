# Planetary Magnetism

Planetary magnetic environments arise from intrinsic dynamos, induced currents, crustal remanence, and solar-wind interaction.

## Key Points

* Magnetic measurements reveal interior conductivity and dynamics.
* Fields and magnetospheres shape charged-particle environments and auroras.
* A world can lack a present global dynamo yet retain crustal magnetization or an induced magnetic response.
