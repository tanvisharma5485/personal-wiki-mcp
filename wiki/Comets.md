# Comets

Comets are volatile-rich small bodies that become active when solar heating drives sublimation.

## Key Points

* Activity creates a coma around the nucleus.
* Ion tails respond strongly to the solar wind.
* Dust tails are shaped by radiation pressure and orbital motion; comet populations also connect to distant reservoirs.
* Halley’s Comet and comet 67P appear as individual examples, while short- and long-period comets connect observed activity to different orbital populations.
