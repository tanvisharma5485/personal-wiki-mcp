# Triton

Triton is Neptune's largest moon and follows a retrograde orbit that points to capture.

## Key Points

* It has a young icy surface.
* A tenuous nitrogen atmosphere surrounds it.
* Active plume-like features show that distant icy worlds can be geologically active.
