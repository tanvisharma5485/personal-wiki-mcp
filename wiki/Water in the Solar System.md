# Water in the Solar System

Water occurs across the Solar System as vapor, exposed or buried ice, hydrated minerals, subsurface oceans, and high-pressure phases.

## Key Points

* Polar cold traps preserve ice on Mercury and the Moon.
* Mars contains widespread water ice and evidence of ancient flowing water.
* Europa, Enceladus, and other icy worlds illustrate that liquid reservoirs can exist beneath frozen surfaces.
