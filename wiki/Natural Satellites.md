# Natural Satellites

Natural satellites are diverse worlds whose evolution is coupled to their planets through gravity, tides, resonances, radiation, and material exchange.

## Key Points

* Tidal heating can sustain activity on small worlds such as Io and Enceladus.
* Several icy moons show evidence for subsurface oceans.
* Captured, regular, and irregular orbits preserve different dynamical histories.
