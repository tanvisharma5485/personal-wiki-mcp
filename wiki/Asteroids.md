# Asteroids

Asteroids are small bodies that preserve evidence of early accretion, heating, differentiation, collision, and space weathering.

## Key Points

* Broad compositional classes include carbon-rich, silicate-rich, and metal-rich objects.
* Dynamical populations include main-belt, Trojan, and near-Earth asteroids.
* Asteroid impacts connect their evolution to cratering and planetary defense.
* The corpus identifies Vesta and Pallas as notable individual asteroids and treats Ceres separately as a dwarf planet in the asteroid belt.
