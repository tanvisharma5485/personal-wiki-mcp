# Solar System Missions

Solar System missions apply flybys, orbiters, landers, rovers, atmospheric probes, impact experiments, and sample return to complementary scientific questions.

## Key Points

* Mercury and Venus missions in the source set include Mariner, MESSENGER, BepiColombo, Venera, and Magellan, together with future Venus mission topics.
* Lunar exploration includes Apollo, the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, Chandrayaan, and Artemis; Mars exploration includes Spirit and Opportunity, Curiosity, Perseverance, and Mars Sample Return.
* Giant-planet exploration includes Pioneer, Voyager, Galileo, Juno, Cassini-Huygens, Europa Clipper, and Dragonfly, with New Horizons extending exploration to Pluto and the Kuiper Belt.
* Dawn investigated Vesta and Ceres, OSIRIS-REx represents asteroid sample return, and DART tested a planetary-defense deflection method.
* Mission status and schedules are time-sensitive; the corpus supports the mission relationships but advises checking current authoritative sources for live status.
