# Small-Body Exploration

Small-body missions investigate asteroids and comets to study primitive material, collisional evolution, and impact hazards.

## Key Points

* Imaging and spectroscopy map shape, surface, and composition.
* Rendezvous and sample return permit detailed study of individual bodies.
* Impact experiments connect exploration with tests of planetary-defense techniques.
