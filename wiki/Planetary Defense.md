# Planetary Defense

Planetary defense concerns detecting, characterizing, and potentially deflecting near-Earth objects that pose impact hazards.

## Key Points

* Near-Earth asteroid observations constrain orbits, sizes, and physical properties.
* Impact history provides context for the consequences and frequency of collisions.
* The DART mission represents an experimental test of asteroid deflection in the source set.
