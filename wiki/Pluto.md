# Pluto

Pluto is a Kuiper Belt dwarf planet with complex geology and volatile ices.

## Key Points

* Its surface includes water-ice mountains and a prominent heart-shaped region.
* Pluto has a thin seasonal atmosphere and five known moons in the source corpus.
* Pluto and its large companion Charon are mutually tidally locked.
