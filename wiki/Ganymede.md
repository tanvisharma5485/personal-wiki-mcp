# Ganymede

Ganymede is the largest moon in the Solar System and the only moon known to generate its own intrinsic magnetic field.

## Key Points

* Its size makes it a major differentiated satellite.
* Its intrinsic field connects satellite geology to dynamo physics.
* Ganymede belongs to Jupiter's four large Galilean moons.
