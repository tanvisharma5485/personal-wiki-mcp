# Planetary Extremes

Planetary records summarize exceptional sizes, temperatures, rotations, gravity, winds, storms, craters, and landforms across the Solar System.

## Key Points

* A record is meaningful only when equivalent quantities and measurement levels are compared.
* Surface and cloud-top temperatures are not interchangeable.
* Extremes are best used as entry points to the physical mechanisms that produce them.
