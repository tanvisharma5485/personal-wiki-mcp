# Titan

Titan is Saturn's largest moon and has a dense nitrogen atmosphere.

## Key Points

* Methane participates in weather and forms hydrocarbon lakes and seas.
* A water-ice crust overlies a likely subsurface ocean.
* Titan combines atmospheric, surface-liquid, ocean-world, and habitability questions.
