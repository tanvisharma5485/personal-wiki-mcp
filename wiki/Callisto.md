# Callisto

Callisto is a heavily cratered Galilean moon with an ancient surface.

## Key Points

* Its preserved craters provide a record of impact history.
* Its interior may contain a deep ocean; an induced magnetic response provides evidence for an electrically conducting subsurface layer.
* Its weak resurfacing contrasts with the intense activity of Io.
