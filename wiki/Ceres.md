# Ceres

Ceres is a dwarf planet in the asteroid belt rich in water-bearing material.

## Key Points

* Its surface contains hydrated minerals.
* Bright salt deposits occur in Occator crater.
* Its location and composition connect dwarf-planet science with asteroid evolution and water distribution.
