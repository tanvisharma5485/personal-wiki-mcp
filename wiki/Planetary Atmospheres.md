# Planetary Atmospheres

Planetary atmospheres are compared through composition, pressure, temperature structure, clouds, circulation, energy sources, and escape.

## Key Points

* Distance from the Sun alone does not determine surface temperature.
* Greenhouse strength explains why Venus can be hotter than a planet closer to the Sun.
* Solar heating, internal heat, rotation, and cloud microphysics help shape circulation and weather.
