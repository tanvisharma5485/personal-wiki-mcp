# Terrestrial Planets

Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars are the Solar System's rock- and metal-rich terrestrial planets.

## Key Points

* Their solid surfaces preserve different mixtures of impact, volcanic, tectonic, erosional, and depositional history.
* Their atmospheres range from Mercury's tenuous exosphere to Venus's massive carbon-dioxide envelope.
* Comparing their water, magnetic fields, interiors, and thermal evolution reveals why superficially similar planets diverged.
