# Planetary Rings

Planetary rings are swarms of orbiting particles, not solid disks.

## Key Points

* Collisions and orbital resonances organize ring structure.
* Embedded or nearby moons can open gaps, confine edges, or supply material.
* Particle composition and electromagnetic forces are especially important for fine dust.
